Account Deletion

Last updated: April 22, 2026

You can request deletion of your TV Tracker account and associated app data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Signed-in app users can open the Menu and choose Delete account.
  2. You can also email contact@tvtracker.me from the address tied to your account.

What gets deleted

Your TV Tracker profile, tracked shows, watch progress, ratings, and account-linked app data that we control.

What may be retained

We may keep limited records that are reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, payment reconciliation, chargeback handling, or legal compliance.

Timeline

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days.

Questions

Contact contact@tvtracker.me.
