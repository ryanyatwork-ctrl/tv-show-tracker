Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

Belleville Systems operates TV Tracker at tvtracker.me. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Your email address via Google OAuth or email magic link.

Library Data: TV shows you track, watch progress, ratings, and notes.

Payment Information: Processed by Stripe. We do not store payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To sync your TV show library across devices
  • To determine your subscription status and unlock paid features
  • To generate personalized show recommendations
  • To look up streaming availability for shows in your library
  • To respond to support inquiries

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL) on AWS US East. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data with: Supabase (database), Stripe (payments), Anthropic (AI recommendations — show titles only, no personal data), TMDB/TVMaze (show metadata — no personal data).

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all data at any time by emailing contact@tvtracker.me. We process deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Children's Privacy

TV Tracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Contact

Questions? contact@tvtracker.me
